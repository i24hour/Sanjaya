01 — Vision

A country does not fit in a spreadsheet.

India already produces the data. It lives in district offices, relief camps, public records, and the phones of people who showed up. Almost none of it shares a shape. By the time it is copied into a deck, the field has moved.

Sanjaya is the instrument for that gap. A way to collect structured truth, reconcile it into one record, and hold a picture that a responsible person can trust.

02 — Platform

What we are building.

Six capabilities, one instrument. This is the product Sanjaya is being formed to deliver: collection at the edge, a record with a source, and a picture someone can act on.

In development

  1. 01

    Field ledger

    Volunteers and operators record what they actually see. Each entry keeps a place, a time, and the evidence behind it, and it can be written when the network is down.

  2. 02

    One record

    Public datasets, institutional feeds, and field reports resolve into a single model of places, assets, events, and the people accountable for them. Duplicates meet. Conflicts stay visible.

  3. 03

    Operational picture

    A live view of condition and change. What is new, what is worsening, and what has gone quiet, for the person who has to decide today.

  4. 04

    Routed work

    A signal becomes an assignment to a named person, with the source attached. The fact and the task travel together.

  5. 05

    Governance

    Access is scoped to a role and a purpose. Every view is remembered. Every fact carries provenance: where it came from, and when.

  6. 06

    Inquiry

    Ask the record in plain language. Answers return with the underlying records attached, so a sentence cannot outrun its evidence.

03 — Principles

How we will behave with what we hold.

  1. India, specifically

    Built for the distances, density, and field conditions of this country. A picture of somewhere else, relocated, will not do.

  2. Truth has a source

    A fact that cannot say where it came from, and when, does not get to look certain.

  3. Access is a duty

    Sight is granted to a role, for a reason, and it is remembered. Holding data is an obligation.

Where this stands

The path.

  1. Now

    In formation. This site states the vision. Conversations are open with people who carry ground truth.

  2. First instrument

    A field ledger and a governed record, serious enough to replace the spreadsheet and the group chat.

  3. The picture

    An operational view for people entrusted to act, at the scale the work actually has.
