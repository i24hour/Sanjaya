              04 — Method

              Collect, resolve, see, act.

              The same cycle Sanjaya is being built to run, applied to one public dataset. Every number on this page traces back to a row with a source.

                What this cannot see

                • Which guest is available. That decides most episodes and is not public, so even the best model's first guess is right only about one time in seven.
                • Topics are tagged automatically from titles and transcripts. Tagging from the transcript alone agrees with the title for about two thirds of episodes.
                • Titles change after release, as the channel tests them, so an episode's topic can shift with its title.
                • Upload dates are only approximate, so news events are not modelled.
