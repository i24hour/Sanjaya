# Raj Shamani's *Figuring Out*: what he talks about, what works, what's next

Built from 640 channel videos: 618 transcripts downloaded, 18 videos with no transcript on YouTube. The analysis covers the 566 numbered podcast episodes (FO1 to FO592), 553 of them with a transcript.

## Key findings

1. **The show has moved from business to the mind.** Psychology & Self-Growth went from 14% to 30% of episodes (last 50 vs the 100 before), while Business, Money and Health shrank.
2. **The language has swung back to English.** English-language episodes went from 71% in FO1–100 down to 7% in FO201–300 (the Hindi, Indian-guest era) and back up to 73% in FO501–600, as foreign guests took over. Hindi and English episodes get about the same median views.
3. **What gets watched isn't what he makes most.** Among topics with 5+ recent episodes, Crime & Dark Side (2.25M median) and Geopolitics & Defence (1.90M) lead; Education & Career trails at 0.53M. Geopolitics is 10% of episodes but 20% of views, while Health is 18% of episodes and 12% of views.
4. **Longer episodes get more views.** Episodes over 2 hours have a 1.60M median, against 0.75M for 1–1.5 hours (likely partly because bigger guests get longer conversations).
5. **Next episode:** most likely Psychology & Self-Growth (19%), then Business & Startups and Health & Fitness. Topic choice is only weakly predictable (14% first-guess accuracy vs 10% for the obvious baseline).
